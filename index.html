Pro Board Privacy Policy

Sinhala and English keyboard for Android · Last updated 31 October 2026

The short version: whatever you type on Pro Board stays on your phone. The keyboard doesn't have any way to send it anywhere, and it works fine with no internet at all. The details are below.

1Who this is about

This policy covers the Android app Pro Board (package name harsha.proboard.codes). I'm an independent developer, not a company, and I built it because I wanted a proper Sinhala keyboard that doesn't spy on people. If anything here is unclear, my email is at the bottom of the page.

2What you type

I never see it. Your messages, searches, passwords and everything else you type are handled by the keyboard on your phone and nowhere else. Word suggestions and the Singlish to Sinhala conversion both run locally.

The part of the app that talks to the internet is kept completely separate from the keyboard itself, so typed text has no route out.

3What stays on your phone

  • Your settings and the theme you picked
  • Words you've added to your personal dictionary, and your recently used emoji
  • Clipboard history, if you turn on the clipboard feature

None of this is uploaded. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it and it's gone.

4What the app does send to my server

Only the home and settings screens do this, and only three small things:

  • A push notification token from Firebase, so I can send you an update notice
  • A Play Integrity token from Google, which just confirms the app is a genuine copy
  • A random install ID. It isn't tied to your name, number or any hardware ID. I use it to count how many installs are still active.

That's the whole list.

5Ads

The app shows ads on its Home screen. You will never see an ad inside the keyboard while typing. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, rough location, and details about your device and how you interact with an ad. That data is handled by Google under their own policies, not by me. You can read how they use it here, and see Google's general privacy policy.

If you'd rather not get personalised ads, open Android Settings, go to Privacy, then Ads, and delete or reset your advertising ID. You can also manage your ad preferences at myadcenter.google.com.

6Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet: update notices and loading ads on the Home screen
  • Notifications: so update notices can show up
  • Vibrate: the key-press buzz
  • Run at startup: only to restart the notice check after your phone reboots
  • Advertising ID: used by the Home screen ads

7Other services the app uses

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google), for push notices
  • Google Play Integrity, to check the app is genuine
  • Google AdMob, for Home screen ads

Firebase Analytics is switched off in this app. I don't sell personal data to anyone.

8Children

Pro Board is a general keyboard and isn't aimed at children under 13. I don't knowingly collect anything from children. If you think a child's information ended up with me, email me and I'll delete it.

9Security

The app only talks to my server over HTTPS, and the login token is kept in Android's secure storage on your phone. No system is perfect, but I've tried to keep the amount of data small so there's very little to lose.

10If this policy changes

I'll update this page and change the date at the top. If something changes in a way that affects you much, I'll mention it in an app update notice too.
